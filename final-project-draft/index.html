Artemis: Returning Humans to the Moon

Follow NASA from the first uncrewed test flight to a lasting outpost at the lunar South Pole.

Welcome to Artemis

Artemis is NASA’s program for sending astronauts on increasingly difficult missions to explore more of the Moon.

The goals are scientific discovery, economic benefits, and a foundation for the first crewed missions to Mars.

This site follows the first five Artemis missions. Artemis I and Artemis II have already flown: an uncrewed test in 2022 and a crewed trip around the Moon in April 2026. Artemis III, planned for 2027, rehearses the docking skills needed for a landing. Artemis IV and Artemis V, both planned for 2028, are lunar surface missions.

Beyond the flights, NASA is building a Moon Base near the lunar South Pole, a place where astronauts will live, work, and explore.

The five missions

  1. Artemis I

    Completed, 2022

    An uncrewed test of the SLS rocket and Orion spacecraft.

  2. Artemis II

    Completed, 2026

    The first crewed flight of Orion, a nearly 10-day trip around the Moon.

  3. Artemis III

    Planned, 2027

    A four-person Earth-orbit test of docking with lander test vehicles.

  4. Artemis IV

    Planned, 2028

    The first crewed Moon landing of the Artemis program.

  5. Artemis V

    Planned, 2028

    A lunar surface mission that starts a yearly pace of landings.
